TuneLatam Privacy Policy

TuneLatam · Android · com.tunelatam.app

ENESPT

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Scope

This policy describes TuneLatam version 1.0. TuneLatam is offline-first: its Tuner, Recorder, Practice tools, reminders, and Metronome remain usable without a TuneLatam cloud account. Some Community, ratings, attribution, and commercial services operate only when the release is configured for them.

Microphone and audio

TuneLatam uses microphone access for real-time instrument tuning and for local practice recordings that you explicitly start.

  • Tuner microphone audio is processed locally while the tuner is active. It is

not recorded or stored, and TuneLatam does not upload or share it.

  • The Practice Recorder saves audio only after you press Record. Recordings

remain in TuneLatam's private app storage until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

  • TuneLatam has no feature that uploads, shares, exports, or publishes Recorder

audio. Recorder capture stops/finalizes when the app leaves the foreground.

  • No microphone PCM, Recorder file, detected pitch/frequency stream, recording

name, or local file path is sent through TuneLatam's network services.

Without microphone permission, live tuning and new Recorder capture cannot analyze sound.

Data stored on the device

TuneLatam stores settings, selected instrument/tuning, custom tunings, Metronome preferences, Practice routines/projects/repertoire/sessions, weekly goal, reminders, recent Practice context, and Recorder metadata in private app storage. These local musical and practice details are not included in TuneLatam's network payloads.

Recorder files and metadata are excluded from TuneLatam's Android cloud-backup and device-transfer rules and are marked excluded from iOS backup where supported. Other preferences and Practice files can be included in platform-managed backups according to device and account settings.

Community and app-generated identifiers

When production Supabase services are configured, TuneLatam can download a curated Community catalog and remote creator avatars. It creates a random installation UUID, which is not an advertising, hardware, Google user, or location identifier. The app can send only these bounded events with that ID: first Preview start, intentional creator-profile view, and intentional outbound-link open. Profile/link events include the relevant creator/link ID and timestamp.

For an eligible creator campaign installed through Google Play, TuneLatam can read the Play Install Referrer and send creator, campaign, click, installation, install timestamp/version, and optional RevenueCat App User ID for attribution. It does not collect generic sessions, scrolling, exact location, contacts, advertising ID, or arbitrary free text.

Supabase tables in this release define no automatic expiry for events, attribution, or commercial reporting. Provider operational logs and transport metadata, such as IP address, follow provider terms and are not copied into TuneLatam's application tables by this code.

Optional ratings account

Community browsing and all musical tools require no TuneLatam account. If you choose to rate a creator, TuneLatam requests Google email and profile authorization and passes Google tokens to Supabase Auth. Supabase stores the resulting authentication identity. A rating stores the Supabase user ID, creator ID, and one value from 1 to 5. Public results expose only counts and, after at least five ratings, an aggregate average.

You can clear an individual rating or delete the ratings account in the app. Account deletion deletes the Supabase authentication user and its ratings and invalidates the associated authenticated session. It does not delete local Practice/Recorder data, Google Play subscriptions, or pseudonymous Community/attribution rows because those are not joined to the ratings identity.

Commercial services and the 7-day Preview

The Tuner stays free. When Premium commercial access is enabled in the distributed build, eligible users receive a seven-day (7 × 24 hours) in-app Preview that begins with their first accepted request for a Premium capability. Preview requires no payment card, is not a Google Play subscription free trial, and does not cause automatic payment or subscription at expiry. After Preview, standalone Metronome access and new Recorder captures require an active Premium entitlement. Existing recordings remain available for browsing, playback and deletion.

TuneLatam uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat for optional monthly, quarterly and annual subscriptions when available. RevenueCat may process an app-generated App User ID, offering and product identifiers, localized prices and currency, CustomerInfo, verified entitlement status and expiry. Google Play manages purchases, renewals and cancellations. Purchases and restores require an explicit user action in an enabled commercial flow. Supplying a public SDK key may initialize RevenueCat to refresh entitlement information, but initialization alone does not charge the user.

Eligible creator installations can send creator, campaign and click attributes to RevenueCat. RevenueCat webhooks can report event/customer aliases, product and entitlement IDs, transactions and timestamps, environment, country, currency and provider price fields to the Supabase backend. TuneLatam disables RevenueCat automatic device-identifier collection and diagnostics in app code. Provider technical processing and retention also follow Google Play and RevenueCat terms.

External actions

Community links, Play rating/subscription pages, sharing, and feedback open an external app after a user action. A Community link logs its bounded outbound event before opening the approved HTTPS URL. The feedback mail composer is prefilled with app version/build/platform; you decide whether to edit and send it. External destinations apply their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Tuner samples are discarded during processing. Local data remains until the relevant delete/reset action, app-data clearing, or uninstall, subject to platform backup restoration. Accepted event queue entries are removed locally; backend event/attribution/revenue rows have no automatic expiry in the current schema. Ratings remain until cleared or the ratings account is deleted. Google/Supabase/Google Play/RevenueCat retention is also governed by those providers and the production operator's policy.

Advertising and prohibited transfers

TuneLatam contains no ads, Firebase, advertising-ID access, location/contact collection, or microphone/Recorder upload. No third-party service receives microphone audio from TuneLatam.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions and the public ratings-account deletion resource, use the verified developer contact and privacy links published on TuneLatam's Google Play listing. Those public values must be configured and verified before release.
