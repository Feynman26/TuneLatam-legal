What account does TuneLatam use?

Core Tuner, Metronome, Recorder and Practice features do not require an account. If Community ratings are enabled and you choose to rate a creator, Google sign-in creates an optional ratings identity through Supabase Auth.

Delete the account from the app

  • Open TuneLatam and go to Community.
  • Open any creator profile and scroll to Ratings.
  • If you previously signed in, select Delete my rating data.
  • Review the confirmation and select Delete rating data.

If ratings are not configured or you are not signed in, that action will not appear. Deletion of the optional ratings account is available when the associated service is configured.

Data deleted and retained

Deleting the ratings account deletes the Supabase authentication identity and its Community ratings and invalidates the authenticated session. It does not delete local Practice data or recordings, unrelated pseudonymous attribution/events, or subscriptions managed by Google Play.

You can delete local recordings in the app. Google Play subscriptions must be canceled separately through Google Play.

Cannot access the app? Request deletion online

You can request deletion of your optional Community ratings account without having the app installed. Send an email to tunelatam.support@gmail.com with the subject “TuneLatam ratings account deletion”.

Request ratings-account deletion by email

Where possible, write from the Google email address you used for Community ratings. If you cannot access that address, explain this in your message so support can arrange an appropriate identity-verification process. We may need to verify account ownership before deleting account information. Never send passwords, login codes, access tokens or audio recordings.

This email starts a support-handled deletion request; it does not delete data automatically. Support will respond about verification and processing. This request concerns only the optional Supabase Community ratings identity and its ratings, not local Practice recordings or Google Play subscriptions.
